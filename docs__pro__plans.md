Plans

There are currently three Pro plans to choose from: Pro Lite, Pro Standard, and Pro Plus.

Pro Lite

This is the entry-level Pro plan. At just $9 / year, it offers some nice improvements over the free Basic experience while still being extremely affordable. For users who don't use custom domains or the more business-oriented features of Pro (like forms and widgets), this is probably a good fit. Here's what it includes:

3 Sites

Build and publish up to 3 sites from a single account.

Premium URLs

Publish sites to unbranded URLs (like .crd.co) and punycode URLs.

No Branding

Publish sites without the "Made with Carrd" branding.

High Quality Images

Publish sites with bigger, higher quality images and backgrounds.

Large Images and Videos

Upload and use images/GIFs up to 16MB and videos up to 64MB.

No Element Limit

Build sites with an unlimited number of elements.

Premium Templates

Access premium starting point templates exclusive to Pro plans.

Custom Templates

Build and use your own custom templates.

Video Uploads

Upload and use your own videos throughout your site (or as its background).

Slideshows

Create fully animated slideshows as backgrounds or standalone elements.

Site Icons

Show custom icons (aka "favicons") in the address bar when visiting your sites.

Share Images

Show custom images (instead of screenshots) when sharing your sites on social media.

QR Codes

Generate and download scannable QR codes for your sites.

Site Transfers

Transfer ownership of sites to other Carrd users.

Sharing

Share access to your sites with other Carrd users.

Select this plan
Pro Standard

This is the mid-tier Pro plan. At $19 / year, this is by far the most popular Pro plan in part because it covers a wide range of use cases: from individuals further branding their sites with custom domains to businesses using forms to collect email addresses and customer feedback. Here's what it includes:

10 Sites

Build and publish up to 10 sites from a single account.

Custom Domain URLs

In addition to premium URLs, publish sites to any custom domains you own (with full SSL support via Let's Encrypt).

No Branding

Publish sites without the "Made with Carrd" branding.

High Quality Images

Publish sites with bigger, higher quality images and backgrounds.

Large Images and Videos

Upload and use images/GIFs up to 16MB and videos up to 64MB.

No Element Limit

Build sites with an unlimited number of elements.

Premium Templates

Access premium starting point templates exclusive to Pro plans.

Custom Templates

Build and use your own custom templates.

Video Uploads

Upload and use your own videos throughout your site (or as its background).

Slideshows

Create fully animated slideshows as backgrounds or standalone elements.

Site Icons

Show custom icons (aka "favicons") in the address bar when visiting your sites.

Share Images

Show custom images (instead of screenshots) when sharing your sites on social media.

QR Codes

Generate and download scannable QR codes for your sites.

Site Transfers

Transfer ownership of sites to other Carrd users.

Sharing

Share access to your sites with other Carrd users.

Forms

Add fully functional contact forms and signup forms (using Mailchimp, Kit, ActiveCampaign, EmailOctopus, and many more) to your sites.

Widgets

Embed third-party widgets from Stripe, PayPal, Gumroad, Typeform, and Facebook.

Embeds

Directly embed your own code (including script tags) as well as external content.

Analytics

Enable optional site analytics to monitor performance using our native, cookie-free analytics service, or a supported third-party provider like Google Analytics, Plausible, and Matomo.

Meta Tags

Add custom meta tags to each of your sites.

Local Fonts

Serve font assets directly from your site.

Select this plan
Pro Plus

This is the top-tier Pro plan. At $49 / year, this plan is built for power users as it offers not only the features of Pro Standard, but also more powerful, developer-friendly features like custom forms, the advanced settings tab, and even the ability to download the unminified sources of the sites you build. Here's what it includes:

25 Sites

Build and publish up to 25 sites from a single account.

Custom Domain URLs

In addition to premium URLs, publish sites to any custom domains you own (with full SSL support via Let's Encrypt).

No Branding

Publish sites without the "Made with Carrd" branding.

High Quality Images

Publish sites with bigger, higher quality images and backgrounds.

Large Images and Videos

Upload and use images/GIFs up to 16MB and videos up to 64MB.

No Element Limit

Build sites with an unlimited number of elements.

Premium Templates

Access premium starting point templates exclusive to Pro plans.

Custom Templates

Build and use your own custom templates.

Video Uploads

Upload and use your own videos throughout your site (or as its background).

Slideshows

Create fully animated slideshows as backgrounds or standalone elements.

Site Icons

Show custom icons (aka "favicons") in the address bar when visiting your sites.

Share Images

Show custom images (instead of screenshots) when sharing your sites on social media.

QR Codes

Generate and download scannable QR codes for your sites.

Site Transfers

Transfer ownership of sites to other Carrd users.

Sharing

Share access to your sites with other Carrd users.

Advanced Forms

In addition to contact and signup forms, build entirely custom forms that integrate with Zapier, Make, n8n and Airtable, send data to URLs, seamlessly incorporate payments (via Stripe Checkout) and more.

Widgets

Embed third-party widgets from Stripe, PayPal, Gumroad, Typeform, and Facebook.

Embeds

Directly embed your own code (including script tags) as well as external content.

Analytics

Enable optional site analytics to monitor performance using our native, cookie-free analytics service, or a supported third-party provider like Google Analytics, Plausible, and Matomo.

Meta Tags

Add custom meta tags to each of your sites.

Local Fonts

Serve font assets directly from your site.

Advanced Settings

Modify advanced element settings like attributes, custom styles, and JS events.

Download Sites

Download the unminified HTML, CSS, JS, and images for any sites you build (note: excludes server-side code).

Redirects

Moving sites to Carrd? Preserve your existing links by redirecting them to sections or external URLs.

Password Protection

Restrict access to a site using a password.

Variables

Insert dynamic content into supported elements.

Site Files

Customize various supporting site files (such as robots.txt and ads.txt).

Framing

Selectively allow your site to be embedded elsewhere using an IFRAME (inline frame).

Update Frequency

Explicitly set the update frequency of a site to help improve its performance.

Canonical URL

Explicitly set the canonical URL of your site.
