Automatically Renewing Your Plan

You can optionally set your Pro plan to auto-renew prior to its end date using your account balance.

Turning On Auto-Renew

Open the Dashboard.

Click your Account avatar in the corner.

Under Current Plan, click Turn On Auto-Renew

Confirm you'd like to turn on auto-renew, then click Turn On Auto-Renew

You're done! Your plan will now attempt to automatically renew prior to its end date using your account balance. Be sure to sufficiently fund your account balance ahead of time (or simply add a saved payment method to your account to automatically fund your balance when needed).

Turning Off Auto-Renew

Open the Dashboard.

Click your Account avatar in the corner.

Under Current Plan, click Turn Off Auto-Renew

Confirm you'd like to turn off auto-renew, then click Turn Off Auto-Renew

You're done! Your plan will no longer automatically renew, though you can still manually renew it at any time by clicking Renew Now on your account page.
