Printing a Receipt

You can obtain a printable receipt for any item purchase by following these steps:

Open the Dashboard.

Click Notifications

Click Library in the sidebar.

Locate the payment for which you'd like to obtain a printable receipt, then click Print Receipt

(Optional) Click the Add Details button to add additional information to the Customer Details section of this particular receipt (for example, your company's registration ID).

Click Print to print a copy of this receipt (or save it as a PDF if supported by your browser).

You're done!
