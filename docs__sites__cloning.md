Cloning a Site

If you find yourself needing to make a copy of a site (as a backup or for the purpose of transferring a copy to another user), follow these steps:

On your Dashboard, locate the site you want to clone.

Click its Clone icon.

Wait a few moments for the copy to be created (this may take a few minutes, particularly for larger sites).

You're done! You should now see a copy of the site on your dashboard. Note that the copy is an entirely separate, standalone site in its own right so it will occupy one of your account's site slots until it's either deleted or transferred to another user.
