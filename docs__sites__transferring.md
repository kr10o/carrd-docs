Transferring a Site

Requires Pro Lite or higher

To transfer ownership of a site to another user, follow these steps:

Open the Dashboard.

Click the Sites tab.

Locate the site you want to transfer, then click its Manage icon. Note that if you only want to transfer a copy of the site, be sure to clone it first.

In the sidebar, click Transfer.

In the Email field, enter the email of the user to whom you want to transfer the site. The email must be linked to an active Carrd account with access to the plan features needed by the site (if any). For example, if the site uses Form elements, the account must be on Pro Standard or higher.

Click Send Transfer Request

You're done ... almost. The user must now either accept your transfer request to complete the transfer, or deny it to cancel the transfer. Note that the user must have a free site slot to accept the transfer.

To cancel a pending transfer, click the site's Manage icon then click Cancel Transfer in the sidebar.
