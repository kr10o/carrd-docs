Working With Custom Templates

Requires Pro Lite or higher

If you're in a situation where you need to build many similar sites but don't want to resort to cloning an existing site several times over, you can instead build and use your own custom templates. Custom templates can be used just like Carrd's own built-in templates, shared with other users, and even made public for anyone to freely use.
