ACCOUNT
Updating Your Profile

Open the Dashboard.

Click your Account avatar in the corner.

In the sidebar, click Profile.

Make changes to your Name, Email, and other profile settings as needed.

(Optional) Change your account avatar by clicking Upload and selecting an image.

Click Update Profile

You're done!
