Setting up Marketing Trackers

Requires Pro Standard or higher

If you're running ad campaigns on platforms like Facebook, you can optionally enable one or more Marketing Trackers on a site to monitor visitors, measure conversions, and improve ad targeting on those platforms.

Enabling a marketing tracker will require the collection of additional data from a site's visitors. Be sure to read and understand the marketing tracker's privacy policies before enabling it.

Open the Dashboard.

Click the Sites tab.

Locate the site in question, then click its Manage icon.

In the sidebar, click Analytics.

Under Marketing Trackers, locate the marketing tracker you want to enable and click On

INSIGHT TAG

PIXEL

PIXEL

Click Enable

You're done! The selected marketing tracker should now be enabled on the site.
