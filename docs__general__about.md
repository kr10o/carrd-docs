About Carrd

Carrd is a free service for building fully responsive one-page sites for pretty much anything. It began life as a small side project by AJ (@ajlkn) but after launching on Twitter and Product Hunt it has since grown into something much bigger – well, bigger than a side project at least :)

If you're curious as to how Carrd came to be, check out Carrd: The Making Of. In the meantime if you have any suggestions, bug reports, or general feedback, feel free to get in touch or simply drop us an email.
