Gifting a Pro Plan

Looking to gift a Pro plan to another user? Here's how:

Open the Dashboard.

Click your Account avatar in the corner.

In the sidebar, click Library.

Click Buy Items

Locate the Pro plan credit you'd like to purchase, then click Buy Now

Check Purchase this item as a gift and enter the recipient of your gift. You can also provide an optional note to include with your gift.

If there's a balance due, select your payment method. If you've chosen to pay with a credit/debit card, please enter your card details (note: avoid using your browser's auto-fill function to do this as doing so may cause issues with the form).

(Optional) If you're paying with a credit/debit card and would like to add it to your account as a saved payment method, check Save this payment method. This is recommended if you plan to make additional gift purchases.

Click Buy Now if you're paying with a credit/debit card or your account balance, or use the PayPal buttons if you're paying with PayPal.

You're done! If the recipient you provided already has an active Carrd account, the Pro plan credit will be gifted directly to that account. If not, a redeem code and instructions will instead be emailed to them. A printable receipt for your gift can also be found by following these instructions.
