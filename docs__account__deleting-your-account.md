Deleting Your Account

To permanently delete a Carrd account and all data associated with it, follow these steps:

Open the Dashboard.

Click your Account avatar in the corner.

In the sidebar, click Delete Account.

Enter your account password for confirmation.

Confirm that you want to do this by checking the confirmation checkbox.

Click Submit Deletion Request

You're done! The account and all data associated with it (including all of its sites, account information, and account history) will be permanently deleted in two weeks. If you want to cancel this delete request, you can do so at any time by simply logging into the account before this date.
