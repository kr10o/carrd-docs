Setting up Site Analytics

Requires Pro Standard or higher

While Carrd sites don't include analytics or tracking by default, you can optionally enable the Site Analytics feature to monitor a site's visitors, traffic sources, and overall performance using either Cloudflare Web Analytics, Fathom Analytics, Google Analytics, Matomo, Plausible, PostHog Web Analytics, Simple Analytics, Umami, or Carrd's native, cookie-free analytics service.

Certain site analytics providers may require the collection of additional data from a site's visitors. Be sure to read and understand the policies of the selected provider before enabling it.

Open the Dashboard.

Click the Sites tab.

Locate the site in question, then click its Manage icon.

In the sidebar, click Analytics.

Under Site Analytics, click Select Provider

Select a site analytics Provider:

Click Enable

You're done! Site analytics should now be enabled on the site using the selected provider, and clicking Manage will link you to that provider to view the site's analytics data.
