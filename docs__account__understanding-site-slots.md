Understanding Site Slots

Carrd allows you to build and publish multiple sites from a single account, the maximum number of which is determined by the number of site slots granted by your plan (see table below). Published sites, drafts, templates, and archived sites all occupy slots, and once you've reached your maximum you won't be able to add, clone, accept transferred sites, or restore previously deleted sites until you either free up slots (by deleting or transferring out sites), or upgrade to a plan with more slots. The table below lists the number of site slots granted by each plan.

Sites moved to your trash folder do not occupy slots. However, restoring sites from your trash folder does require having sufficient slots available for them.
