PRO
Features

The following is a list of all features exclusive to our Pro plans:

10 Sites
Build and publish up to 10 sites from a single account.
100 Sites
Build and publish up to 100 sites from a single account.
1000 Sites
Build and publish up to 1000 sites from a single account.
25 Sites
Build and publish up to 25 sites from a single account.
250 Sites
Build and publish up to 250 sites from a single account.
50 Sites
Build and publish up to 50 sites from a single account.
500 Sites
Build and publish up to 500 sites from a single account.
Advanced Forms
Build custom forms that integrate with Zapier, Make, n8n and Airtable, send data to URLs, seamlessly incorporate payments and more.
Advanced Settings
Modify advanced element settings like attributes, custom styles, and JS events.
Analytics
Enable optional site analytics to monitor performance using our native, cookie-free analytics service, or a supported third-party provider like Google Analytics, Plausible, and Matomo.
Bigger Sites
Build sites with an unlimited number of elements.
Canonical URL
Explicitly set the canonical URL of your site.
Custom Domain URLs
Publish sites to any custom domains you own (with full SSL support via Let's Encrypt).
Custom Meta Tags
Add custom meta tags to each of your sites.
Custom Templates
Build and use your own custom templates.
Download Sites
Download the unminified HTML, CSS, JS, and images for any sites you build (note: excludes server-side code).
Embeds
Directly embed your own code (including script tags) as well as external content.
Forms
Add fully functional contact forms and signup forms (using Mailchimp, Kit, ActiveCampaign, EmailOctopus, and many more) to your sites.
Framing
Selectively allow your site to be embedded elsewhere using an IFRAME (inline frame).
High Quality Images
Publish sites with bigger, higher quality images and backgrounds.
Large Images and Videos
Upload and use images/GIFs up to 16MB and videos up to 64MB.
Local Fonts
Serve font assets directly from your site.
No Branding
Publish sites without the "Made with Carrd" branding.
Password Protection
Restrict access to a site using a password.
Premium Templates
Access premium starting point templates exclusive to Pro plans.
Premium URLs
Publish sites to unbranded URLs (like .crd.co) and punycode URLs.
QR Codes
Generate and download scannable QR codes for your sites.
Redirects
Moving sites to Carrd? Preserve your existing links by redirecting them to sections or external URLs.
Share Images
Show custom images (instead of screenshots) when sharing your sites on social media.
Sharing
Share access to your sites with other Carrd users.
Site Files
Customize various supporting site files (such as robots.txt and ads.txt).
Site Icons
Show custom icons (aka "favicons") in the address bar when visiting your sites.
Site Transfers
Transfer ownership of sites to other Carrd users.
Slideshows
Create fully animated slideshows as backgrounds or standalone elements.
Update Frequency
Explicitly set the update frequency of a site to help improve its performance.
Variables
Insert dynamic content into supported elements.
Video Uploads
Upload and use your own videos throughout your site (or as its background).
Widgets
Embed third-party widgets from Stripe, PayPal, Gumroad, Typeform, and Facebook.
