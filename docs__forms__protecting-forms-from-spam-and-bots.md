Protecting Forms from Spam and Bots

Requires Pro Standard or higher

While forms automatically provide baseline mitigation against spam and bots, you can take this a step further by adding either Cloudflare Turnstile, Google reCAPTCHA v2, or hCaptcha to your form. Here's how:

Click Add Element

Click Form to create a new Form element.

Scroll down to Options, then select a Spam Protection provider:

While only one provider can be selected per form, the same provider and keys can be reused across multiple forms on the same site.

(Optional) Click Done to close the properties panel.

You're done! Your form is now protected against spam and bots using the provider you selected.
